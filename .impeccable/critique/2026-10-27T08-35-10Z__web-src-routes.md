## Remaining priority issues

### 1. **[P2] Initial settings-load failure has no in-page Retry**

**Where:** `/settings`, initial `GET /api/settings`.

A synthetic 503 left the form absent and displayed an error without an action. Browser reload recovered it. Operators should not need navigation or reload to recover from a transient fetch failure.

**Remedy:** Add Retry to the load-error banner with loading feedback.  
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`.

### 2. **[P2] Form summary repeats long instructions without a bound**

**Where:** Global Settings → Form summary.

Long instructions appear in both the editable field and an unbounded summary. On mobile, the duplicate reading surface follows the full form and adds substantial scrolling.

**Remedy:** Use a bounded preview with explicit expansion; retain the complete editable text.  
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`.
